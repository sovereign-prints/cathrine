Workflow Overview

Quote to Invoice Process:

  1. Customer submits quote request
  2. Review quote in "New Quote" tab
  3. Edit quote details and pricing
  4. Approve quote → moves to Projects (Orders in Progress)
  5. Track project in Projects tab
  6. Mark project as Complete
  7. Generate & edit invoice for final costs
  8. Project appears in Orders Complete
